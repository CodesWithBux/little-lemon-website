About Little Lemon

Thirty years of Mediterranean hospitality in the heart of Pretoria.

Since 1995

A taste of the Mediterranean, in Pretoria

Little Lemon opened its doors in Arcadia in 1995, started by two friends who missed the sun-soaked food of the coast. Three decades later we're still family-run, still serving recipes passed down through generations — and still welcoming first-timers and regulars alike.

We believe booking a table should be as relaxed as the meal itself. That's why we built a reservation system that takes less than a minute, sends you a written confirmation, and lets you get on with your day.
